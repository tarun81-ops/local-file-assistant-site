Why local

Intelligence you own, not rent

Cloud assistants need your files on their servers. A small model on your own PC needs nothing but the PC you already have.

Your files never leave

Trust & accuracy
Can I trust what a small model says?

Treat it like a fast research assistant, not an oracle. It can still make mistakes, which is why each answer shows the file and page it used, and why a claim that is not in the file is marked. Open the source when the answer matters. Files only mode adds a stricter check.

Lease ends 31 March 2027 ✓ lease_agreement.pdf · p.4
! "Deposit is refundable" not verified
What happens when the answer isn't in my files?

With Files only on, it says it cannot back the answer with one of your files instead of answering from general knowledge.

Will it change or delete my files?

Only after you approve a proposal. Removed files go to the Recycle Bin. Reading your files never changes them.

Proposal: delete 3 duplicate photos
ApproveSkip
Does my data train a model?

No. The model runs on your PC and your files are never uploaded to us, so there is nothing for us to train on.

Setup & hardware
Which build should I pick?

Pick OpenVINO on an Intel PC, especially one with a Core Ultra processor, because it can use the graphics chip or the NPU. Pick Ollama if you already run Ollama or your PC does not have Intel graphics.

How much memory and disk does it need?

16 GB of RAM is recommended. The models are about 1.9 GB on first run, or about 1.1 GB on PCs with less RAM.

What is a small language model?

A compact AI model, a few gigabytes in size, built to run on ordinary hardware. It is not as broad as the biggest cloud models, but for answering questions from your own documents it is fast, private and good enough, and it shows its sources.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. The installers are for Windows 11.

Day to day
Does it need the internet?

Only to download a model and to check for updates. After that it answers offline.

How do I update it?

It checks once a day and shows "Update ready, restart?". Nothing installs until you click. The check can be switched off in Settings.
